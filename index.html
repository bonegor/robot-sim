Robot Sim

Snap building blocks together with joints, decide which joints flop around and which ones follow a signal, then let physics take over.

Building

  1. Where? Click a blue dot on the robot. Each dot is a snap point; the yellow arrow shows which way the new part will stick out.
  2. Joint: pick how the new part connects — a hinge like a knee, a ball like a hip, an axle for a wheel…
  3. Part: click a building block. It snaps on, and the next dot is pre-selected at its far end, so chains are quick.

Click any part to change its size, its joint and how each joint moves. R turns the selected part's joint by 90°, Delete removes it (and everything hanging off it), Ctrl+Z undoes. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

Free or driven?

Every joint has one or more degrees of freedom (a hinge has one, a ball joint three). Each one gets a drive:

  • Free — floppy, swings under gravity and contact.
  • Spring — passive but elastic, pulls back to its rest position like a tendon.
  • Servo — moves to the position its signal asks for.
  • Motor — turns or slides at the speed its signal asks for (wheels!).

A driven joint's signal can be a wave (sine, square, triangle, saw, pulse — shift the phase between legs to make a gait), the keyboard, a logic formula (time, keys, sensors such as roll, angle("knee"), touching("paw"), comparisons, if ? then : else…), a shared channel of the robot, or a constant.

Simulating

Press ▶ Simulate. Keys you hold go to the robot, clicking a part pokes it, and drives and signals can be tuned live in the inspector (choose Plot this to see target vs. actual). Esc goes back to building.

Robots are saved in your browser automatically; Save/Open exchange them as JSON blueprints.
